Delete your Lumen data (keep your account)

Lumen – AI Photo Editor · published by Porcupine

You can delete the photos you sent to Lumen's cloud without deleting your account or losing your credits. To delete everything, including your account and credits, see Delete your account.

Option 1: in the app

  1. Open Lumen and tap Settings.
  2. Under Privacy, tap Delete my cloud data.
  3. Read the summary and tap Delete.

Option 2: by email

Email porcupinebase@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Lumen data" from the Google account email you signed in with, and tell us which data you want deleted. We may ask you to confirm the request, and we will act on it within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Photos you uploaded for Magic Erase or Enhance, and their results, in our cloud storage.
  • Your cloud job history (which tool was used, status and cost of each job).

Cloud jobs that are still running are stopped. A job that is already being processed is not refunded, and results that used credits but have not reached your phone yet are lost.

What is kept

Your account, your credit balance and your credit history (purchases, starter credits, credits used and refunded) stay, so you can keep using your credits.

Automatic deletion

Even if you do nothing, uploaded photos and results are deleted automatically, normally within 24 hours and in any case within 48 hours. Photos you saved to your phone's gallery stay on your phone; delete them there if you wish.
