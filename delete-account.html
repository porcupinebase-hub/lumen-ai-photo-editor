Delete your Lumen account

Lumen – AI Photo Editor · published by Porcupine

You can delete your Lumen account and its data at any time, from the app or by sending us a request.

Option 1: in the app

  1. Open Lumen and tap Settings.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  3. Read the summary and confirm.

Your account is deleted right away. If you only want to remove the photos you sent to our cloud and keep your credits, use Settings → Privacy → Delete my cloud data instead.

Option 2: by email

If you no longer have the app, email porcupinebase@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Lumen account" from the Google account email you signed in with. We may ask you to confirm the request, and we will delete the account within 30 days.

If you never signed in with Google, your account is anonymous and we cannot identify it from an email address. Uninstalling the app removes your access to it; its uploaded photos are deleted automatically within 48 hours.

What is deleted

  • Your account (Google sign-in link or anonymous ID).
  • Your credit balance and credit history. Remaining credits are lost and cannot be refunded or restored.
  • Photos and results in our cloud storage, and your cloud job history.

What we keep

Records of completed purchases (product, date, order status), no longer linked to your account, for as long as needed for fraud prevention, refunds and tax law. Google Play keeps its own purchase records under the Google Privacy Policy.

Photos you saved to your phone's gallery stay on your phone; delete them there if you wish.
